## Glyphs

All three are 24 × 24 filled paths drawn by `components/logos/Logo.tsx` (`decorative`: the tile's text names it),
`currentColor`, muted at rest and accent on hover and focus.

- **GitHub**: `logoPaths.github` from the generated `paths.ts` (simple-icons 16.9.0, P1's generator, D78).
- **LinkedIn**: simple-icons dropped LinkedIn's mark at the brand's request, so 16.9.0 has no `siLinkedin`. The
  "in" glyph is authored by hand in `components/logos/extra.ts`: a rounded square with the "i" (dot and stem) and the
  "n" cut out. It follows the shape of LinkedIn's own "in" bug but isn't their artwork.
- **Mail**: an authored envelope (solid body, the flap's V cut out) in `extra.ts`.

`extra.ts` entries carry `fillRule: 'evenodd'` (their cut-outs are separate subpaths); `Logo` takes either key and
passes the rule through, so the generated paths keep the default `nonzero` they were drawn for. `paths.ts` stays
generated and untouched.
